ABOUT US

At House of Sausage, we believe great food starts with bold flavor, quality ingredients, and passion for craftsmanship. Inspired by traditional sausage-making techniques and modern street-food culture, we create premium sausages that bring people together — whether it's on a pizza, in a sandwich, or served fresh off the grill.

From smoky pepperoni to juicy chicken sausages, every product is crafted to give customers an unforgettable bite every time. We are more than just a sausage company — we are building a food brand that represents creativity, consistency, and innovation in Nepal's growing food industry.

Our Mission

To deliver rich, satisfying flavors using carefully selected meats, signature spice blends, and uncompromising quality standards. With a focus on taste, hygiene, and premium quality, House of Sausage aims to become the go-to name for sausage lovers, restaurants, cafes, and pizza brands across the country.
